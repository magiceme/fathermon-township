# v0.6 feature changelog

- Animated SayverOcean splash and township title diorama; New Game, Continue, Load, Settings, Credits; original-art character selection, town naming and welcome colors; cinematic arrival views.
- Three independently saved townships, timestamps and previews, validated JSON import/export, automatic v0.3–v0.5 migration without replacing the original saved values.
- Ten rounded 3D character meshes matching the original palettes, silhouettes and distinguishing features. Walk, idle/breathing, limb, wing/fin/tail and interaction motion are procedural. Original drawings remain selectable in the 3D world and are used by the fallback, portraits and selection screen.
- World bounds −96…160 by −96…144: approximately 38.5 times the original town area. The saved 42×38 town retains its coordinates. Forests, a winding river, walkable bridges, far ridgelines, local/world maps, and region-specific restoration stops support exploration.
- Larger trees relative to small creatures; rounded canopy geometry, soft ground shadows, gradual day/night light, haze, drizzle and fireflies; smooth follow camera; cached scenery and per-species GPU buffers; lower-detail settings and context-loss recovery.
- Workshop furniture: the existing seven indoor recipes plus handmade seats, picnic tables and flower troughs with four finishes and three motifs. Gathering earns Inspiration, consumed by currently placed handmade designs. Valid placement charges materials; move, rotate, remove, undo and redo remain available. Close-up previews show the actual meshes.
- Modular home workshop: material-cost porches, chimneys and a garden wing; attached geometry and collisions; a wing expands the room from 30 to 40 furniture spaces. Existing exterior, roof, door, window and interior choices remain.
- Resident-specific furniture wishes and useful community spaces. Physical layouts, friendship and invitations determine project readiness. Celebrations reward once; a cooked meal can host one gathering per project each day and affect resident routines.
- A five-star township rating based on real homes, permanent residents, furniture, projects and discoveries. The Meadow Guesthouse unlocks after the restored workshop and first star. Invite a temporary visitor, meet them, then offer a vacant cottage for permanent residence; uninvited guests leave at the next day.
- Workshop field lessons consume materials and require town stars. Trailcraft, Ridge stepping and River surveying open woodland, ridge and eastern routes. Discover areas and repair physical resting places without replacing Chapters 1–3.
- Categorized backpack, full town/world maps, consistent dialogs, keyboard focus handling, readable phone layouts, adjustable joystick/interface size, touch/mouse camera gestures, keyboard and optional gamepad input.
- Chapter 4 remains a continuation boundary. No new evolution relationships or major story canon are introduced.

## Additional beta systems

- Hot Sunstone Flats, cold Frostleaf Highlands, and Fernveil Jungle, each with materials, harvest seeds, distinctive vegetation and atmospheric effects. New crops need three watered stages and can become shared meals.
- Learned swimming, diving and glider flight, water-only travel, an underwater riverbed with pearl gathering, safe landing, and actual craftable river bridges for walking routes.
- Thirteen indoor furniture recipes, biome-material pieces, moving/rotating/refinishing furniture, interactive 3D rooms, and Use mode: sleeping, sitting, eating, cooking, reading, diary writing, houseplant/aquarium care, storage and lamp switching.
- 600 authored contextual conversation entries for the original ten Fathermon, recent-line memory, questions by topic, plus eight authored lines for each of 30 additional guest concepts. Original evolution relationships are untouched.
- Thirty additional guests with distinct models, portraits, routines, home preferences, gifts/favors and paired conversations; one shared hotel visitor invitation per day; 32 home plots across eight neighborhoods.
- 62 collection entries: 20 plants, 15 insects, 15 fish, and 12 curios. Habitat, hour, weather, seasons and rarity influence availability. Physical specimens are consumed consistently by food, gifts, shops and donations.
- Crafted bug net and fishing pole; equipped tools, touch actions, Space shortcut, movement/camera joysticks, controller camera input and Chromebook keyboard/trackpad controls.
- Museum, park, laboratory, pizzeria, library and greenhouse, each buildable and upgradeable to level three through real material costs and missions. Larger levels have larger physical footprints and service benefits. Daily shops rotate seeds and limited furniture.
- Three action-based daily quests, 26 achievements including home activities, persistent one-time reward claims, birthday preferences, eight real-date festivals, seasonal celebrations and collectible keepsakes.
- Four 28-day town seasons, seasonal vegetation/colors and harvest bonuses, regional clear/cloud/rain/storm/mist/snow weather, forecasts, rain watering and rain ambience.
- Original procedural title/town music, adjustable music/effect volume, interaction sounds and footsteps, all started after a player gesture.
